About me

I'm a web developer who enjoys turning static designs into responsive, working interfaces. Most of my practice comes from rebuilding real-world sites — streaming platforms, video apps, landing pages — to understand how layout, spacing, and interaction actually hold together at every screen size. I care about clean markup, readable CSS, and pages that feel considered rather than assembled. Right now I'm building out this portfolio project by project, and looking for opportunities to keep learning on real briefs.
